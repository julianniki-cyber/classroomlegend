Questions

Before you partner with us

What's the difference between Gold and Diamond?

Gold gives you 10 in-post ads and a dedicated thank-you post each month. Diamond doubles the ads to 20 a month, includes the thank-you post, and adds a one-page website for your business designed by our team.

What is included in the Diamond one-page website?

A mobile-friendly page with your business name and logo, what you offer, your address and timings, call and WhatsApp buttons, your Teachers' Privilege offer if you have one, and your Diamond Partner badge. We'll talk through the domain name and hosting with you when you sign up.

Can I choose which teacher my ad appears with?

We match partners with teachers where it fits, for example a bookshop with a Tamil teacher or a sports shop with a PE teacher. Tell us your preference and we'll try to follow it.

What is the Teachers' Privilege?

An optional discount you offer to all teachers, such as 20% off on selected items with a school ID card. We feature it in your ads, and teachers who come in can mention Classroom Legend so you can see the results.

When do I pay, and how?

Payment is in advance, by UPI or bank transfer, after we call you to confirm your package. You'll get a numbered invoice. Payments aren't refunded once posts are published.

Does the teacher endorse my business?

No. You support the page, and the teacher is never shown as endorsing any business. That's part of why families trust the posts.
